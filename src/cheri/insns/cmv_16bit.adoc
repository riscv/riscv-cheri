Expansion::
`{CMV} {cd}, {cs1}`

NOTE: `{cs2}` in the 16-bit encoding is mapped to `{cs1}` in the expansion due to the different encoding formats.
 This follows the same pattern as `C.MV` expanding to `MV` (`ADDI rd, rs1, 0`).

NOTE: `C.MV` is remapped to copy YLEN-bit registers for {cheri_base_ext_name}. The mnemonic is changed to avoid ambiguity about whether the copy is XLEN or YLEN bits.
